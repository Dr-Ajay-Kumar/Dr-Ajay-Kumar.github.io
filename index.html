About Me

Academic & Professional Biography

Bridging 27 years of telecom engineering leadership with cutting-edge academic pedagogy and computer science research.

Career Overview

Prof. (Dr.) Ajay Kumar is a seasoned academic leader, researcher, and senior telecommunications professional currently serving as Professor & Head of Department – Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at RTC Institute of Technology (RTCIT), Ranchi. He also serves as the national Vice President of The Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE), New Delhi, and an elected Governing Council Member (2019–2022, 2023–2026).

He holds an awarded Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Usha Martin University (NAAC A accredited) with Grade A+, defending his doctoral dissertation on "IoT Based Vehicle Accident Monitoring System using Raspberry Pi". He completed his M.Tech in CSE from IETE New Delhi with First Division (72%), which is recognized by the Government of India as equivalent to M.Tech (CS) for superior central posts, following a B.E./B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (First Division).

Prior to his current role, Dr. Kumar served as Associate Professor at Sarala Birla University, Ranchi (2024–2025) and Assistant Professor at Usha Martin University (2023–2024). He dedicated 27 years of exemplary executive service to the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and BSNL, retiring as Assistant General Manager (AGM). In BSNL, he held pivotal administrative portfolios including AGM (HR) managing human resources, postings, and promotions for ~3,000 employees across Jharkhand Telecom Circle, AGM (Admin), and AGM (Network Planning - CFA).

His technical contributions include serving in the core team that indigenously developed the DOTSOFT software platform, managing India’s National Internet Backbone (NIB) A1 Node in AP Telecom, and serving as Faculty Head for Broadband & Access Technology at the Advanced Regional Telecom Training Centre (ARTTC), Ranchi. His foundational career includes 13 years of service with the Corps of Electrical and Mechanical Engineers (EME), Indian Army (1979–1992), including 5 years as Technical Instructor at the Military College of EME (MCEME), Secunderabad.

Awarded Doctoral Dissertation

Ph.D. Thesis: IoT Based Vehicle Accident Monitoring System using Raspberry Pi

Doctoral research completed at Usha Martin University (NAAC A Grade), awarded on 10th February 2023 with Grade A+. The research develops an intelligent edge-computing hardware and sensor model utilizing Raspberry Pi 4, MEMS accelerometer sensors, GPS geofencing, and GSM cellular telemetry to monitor vehicular kinematics in real-time, instantly classify collision events, and autonomously dispatch geo-tagged emergency distress signals to emergency dispatch nodes.
